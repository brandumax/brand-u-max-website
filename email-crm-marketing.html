EMAIL & CRM FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Email & CRM Marketing

What is email marketing and why is it important?

Email marketing is the practice of sending targeted emails to your customers to build relationships, drive repeat purchases and maximize customer lifetime value. It's one of the highest-ROI marketing channels because your audience has already opted in.

What is a CRM and how does it help?

A CRM (customer relationship management) system organizes your customer data, tracks interactions and automates workflows. It helps you segment your audience and send the right message at the right time, boosting retention and sales.

How much does email marketing cost?

Costs depend on your audience size, email volume and platform. Most businesses see a strong return because email campaigns are inexpensive to run compared to paid ads. Brand U Max offers tailored email and CRM plans.

What is abandoned cart email and does it work?

An abandoned cart email automatically follows up with customers who added items to their cart but didn't purchase. It's highly effective — recovering significant lost revenue by reminding and incentivizing customers to complete their purchase.
