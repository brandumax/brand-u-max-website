Business Growth FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Business Development

What is business development?

Business development is the strategy of creating long-term growth for a company through partnerships, new markets, new revenue channels and customer relationships. It's different from sales — it focuses on building the systems, partnerships and channels that generate sustainable revenue growth.

How does business development differ from sales and marketing?

Sales closes deals with existing prospects. Marketing attracts and nurtures those prospects. Business development creates the broader growth strategy — new markets, partnerships and revenue models — that sales and marketing then execute against.

How can a business growth consultant help my business in India?

A business growth consultant studies your market, competitors and operations to find untapped opportunities, then builds and executes a strategy to capture them — new partnerships, revenue channels and market expansion — so you grow faster and more profitably.

How fast can a business grow with the right strategy?

With the right business development strategy, companies can see measurable new revenue channels and partnership wins within the first 3-6 months, with compounding growth over the following years.
