Sachin Tendulkar Without DRS: How Many More Runs Could He Have Scored?

September 30, 2026 · 3 min read

← Back to Blog

Sachin Tendulkar and Virat Kohli are both legends of Indian cricket, each dominating a different era with extraordinary batting records.

One fascinating "what if" is Sachin's career without the Decision Review System (DRS). During most of his playing years, a wrong LBW or caught-behind decision could not be challenged.

Some unofficial projections suggest that if disputed dismissals had been successfully reviewed and Sachin had continued those innings, his career total could potentially have reached around 36,500-37,200 international runs, compared with his official 34,357 runs. His century tally could also have moved beyond 100.

These figures are hypothetical projections, not official statistics, because nobody can know how each innings would have developed after a corrected decision.

Virat Kohli's generation has benefited from DRS, advanced analytics and modern cricket technology.

Different eras. Different conditions. Different advantages.

But one thing remains unchanged — Sachin Tendulkar and Virat Kohli are both true cricket legends. 🇮🇳🏏
