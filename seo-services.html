SEO FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About SEO

What is SEO and why does my business need it?

SEO, or search engine optimization, is the process of improving your website to rank higher on Google and other search engines for keywords your customers search. For Indian businesses, local SEO helps you appear when nearby customers search for services like yours, bringing free, consistent organic traffic that converts to leads and sales.

How long does it take to see SEO results?

Most businesses start seeing measurable ranking and traffic improvements within 2 to 4 months, with significant compounding results by 6 to 12 months. SEO is a long-term investment — every month of optimization builds your authority and organic growth.

How much does SEO cost in India?

SEO pricing at Brand U Max depends on your goals, competition and scope of services. We offer tailored SEO plans and recommend a free audit so you know exactly what you need and what it will cost before committing.

What is the difference between SEO, SEM and PPC?

SEO drives free organic traffic by improving your site's ranking over time. SEM (search engine marketing) is the broader term that includes paid ads. PPC (pay-per-click) is the paid side — you pay each time someone clicks. The best strategy combines long-term SEO with targeted PPC for fastest growth.

Do you do local SEO for Indian businesses?

Yes. We specialize in local SEO across India — optimizing your Google Business Profile, local keywords, reviews and citations so you rank in local searches and Google Maps results.
