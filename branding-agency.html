Branding FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Branding

What is branding and why is it important?

Branding is the process of creating a distinct identity for your business — including your logo, colors, messaging, voice and customer experience. Strong branding builds trust, makes you memorable, and helps customers choose you over competitors, which directly supports growth and revenue.

How much does branding cost in Coimbatore?

Branding cost depends on the scope — from a simple logo to a full brand strategy and identity system. Brand U Max offers flexible branding packages and recommends a free brand audit so you know exactly what package suits your goals and budget.

How long does it take to build a brand identity?

A complete brand identity (strategy, logo, colors, guidelines) typically takes 3-6 weeks. It depends on the complexity and how quickly we align on the direction.

Is branding different from marketing?

Yes. Branding defines who you are and your identity — the foundation. Marketing is how you promote and communicate that identity to drive sales. Great branding makes your marketing more effective and memorable.
