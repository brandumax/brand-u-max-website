Marketplace FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Marketplace Onboarding

What is marketplace onboarding?

Marketplace onboarding is the full process of getting your brand live and selling on e-commerce and quick-commerce platforms like Myntra, Amazon, Flipkart, Blinkit, Nykaa and Ajio. It includes seller registration, documentation, catalog setup, compliance and brand-page creation.

How long does it take to get onboarded on a marketplace?

Onboarding timelines vary by platform, but most marketplace listings can go live within 2-6 weeks once your documentation and catalog are ready. Brand U Max handles the entire process to speed it up.

Which marketplaces should I sell on?

The right marketplaces depend on your product category and brand. We help you choose the platforms that match your audience and category best — whether that's Myntra and Ajio for fashion, Nykaa for beauty, or Amazon and Flipkart for general retail and quick-commerce like Blinkit.

What does marketplace management include?

Marketplace management covers day-to-day operations: listing optimization, ad management, sale-event planning, SKU profitability, catalog compliance and monthly reporting so your storefront keeps performing.
