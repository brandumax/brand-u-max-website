Social Media FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Social Media Marketing

Why does my business need social media marketing?

Social media marketing helps you reach and engage your target audience where they spend their time, build brand awareness, and turn followers into loyal customers. It's essential for brand building, community trust and driving traffic to your website or store.

How much does social media marketing cost in India?

The cost depends on the platforms, content volume and whether you want paid ads. Brand U Max offers flexible social media plans and recommends a free audit to find the right package for your budget and goals.

How long does it take to grow social media following?

With consistent, strategic content you can see meaningful follower growth and engagement within 2-3 months. Paid social campaigns can accelerate growth much faster. Real, loyal growth comes from consistency plus targeted paid boosts.

Should I use both organic and paid social media?

Yes. Organic content builds community and trust, while paid boosts expand your reach and drive conversions. A balanced strategy uses both to grow your audience and your bottom line together.
