PPC FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About PPC

What is PPC advertising?

PPC, or pay-per-click advertising, is a model where you pay a fee each time someone clicks your ad. You bid on keywords, and your ad appears in search results or on social platforms. With strong campaign management, PPC delivers fast, measurable ROI by putting your brand in front of high-intent buyers immediately.

How much should I spend on Google Ads?

Your ad budget depends on your industry, goals and competition. We recommend starting with a budget you're comfortable allocating for at least 90 days so we can gather data and optimize. Our team builds campaigns around your budget to maximize return on ad spend.

How fast will I see results from PPC?

Unlike SEO which takes months, PPC can drive traffic and leads within days of campaign launch. However, it typically takes 2-4 weeks of testing and optimization to reach stable, efficient performance and lower your cost per lead.

What's the difference between SEO and PPC?

SEO builds free organic traffic over time, while PPC delivers immediate paid traffic. SEO is a long-term asset; PPC is fast and flexible. Most successful brands use both — SEO for sustainable growth and PPC for immediate visibility.
