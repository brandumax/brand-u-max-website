CRO FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Conversion Rate Optimization

What is conversion rate optimization (CRO)?

CRO is the practice of improving your website and landing pages to turn more of your existing visitors into leads or customers. It focuses on removing friction, testing changes and using data to increase conversions without increasing ad spend.

How much can CRO improve my conversion rate?

Businesses typically see conversion rate improvements of 20% to 50% or more through systematic A/B testing and funnel optimization. The exact uplift depends on your current funnel and the opportunities we identify.

How long does it take to see CRO results?

You can start seeing improvements within 4-8 weeks as we run and analyze tests. CRO is an ongoing process of testing and refining that compounds over time.

What's the difference between CRO and SEO?

SEO brings people to your site; CRO converts the people already there. SEO grows your traffic while CRO increases the value of that traffic. Both work best together.
