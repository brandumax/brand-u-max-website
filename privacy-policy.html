Privacy Policy

Last updated: 19 September 2026

This policy explains what information Brand U Max (“we”, “us”) collects through brandumax.com and how we use it. We are based at 49, Labours Colony, Venkateswara Nagar, Koundampalayam, Coimbatore – 641030, Tamil Nadu, India.

Analytics (Google Analytics 4)

With your consent, we use Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed, approximate location, device type and which buttons are clicked. Google Analytics sets cookies and sends this usage data to Google. We do not load Google Analytics until you click “Accept” on the cookie banner. If you click “Decline”, no analytics cookies are set. Your choice is stored in your browser (localStorage) so we do not ask again. To change your choice, clear this site’s data in your browser and reload the page.

WhatsApp enquiries

Our lead form and buttons open WhatsApp with a pre-filled message. The name and email you type into the lead form are placed in that message, and nothing is sent until you press send in WhatsApp. When you message us, WhatsApp shares your phone number and message with us. We use it only to reply to your enquiry and, if you agree, to follow up. WhatsApp’s own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it.

Contact form

When you submit the contact form we receive your name, email address, company name (optional) and message. The form is processed by Formspree (formspree.io), which delivers the submission to us by email. We use this information only to respond to you and to provide the services you ask about.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not use your information for automated decision-making.
  • We do not knowingly collect information from children.

How long we keep information

We keep enquiry emails and WhatsApp conversations for as long as needed to handle your request and for our business records, then delete them on request or when they are no longer needed. Analytics data is retained according to our Google Analytics settings.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to stop contacting you. Email info@brandumax.com and we will respond promptly.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
